SignalR_Hub (ChatHub @ /chathub):
  - OnConnectedAsync     → tracks presence, broadcasts UserWentOnline
  - OnDisconnectedAsync  → tracks presence, broadcasts UserWentOffline when last conn drops

                        FriendRequestAccepted, AddedToGroup, OutboxDeadLetter
